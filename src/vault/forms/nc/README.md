# NC protective-order forms

Blank, official North Carolina AOC forms for the DVPO flow. Public documents.
**Blank templates only — never commit a form filled with a survivor's
information.**

| Form | Title | Status |
|---|---|---|
| AOC-CV-303 | Complaint for Domestic Violence Protective Order | mapped (`form.py`) |
| AOC-CV-609 | Affidavit as to Status of Minor Child | not yet mapped (one per child, if custody sought) |

## Notes

- AOC-CV-303 (G.S. 50B-1 et seq.) covers parties, the 50B relationship basis,
  the abuse statement, firearms, and a 1-17 list of relief requested.
- The NC intake section fills the NC-specific items — NC's relief list (1-17) is
  its own, distinct from the other states'. See `coverage.md`.
- If temporary child custody is sought, an **AOC-CV-609** affidavit is required
  per child — not yet mapped.
- Drop the official blank fillable PDFs here for lea-be-core's renderer. Source:
  nccourts.gov forms.
