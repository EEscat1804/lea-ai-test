# PA protection-from-abuse forms

Blank, official Pennsylvania PFA forms. Public documents. **Blank templates
only — never commit a form filled with a survivor's information.**

| Form | Title | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Petition for Protection from Abuse | 23 Pa.C.S. ch. 61 | mapped (`form.py`) |
| Attachment A | Firearms/Weapons/Ammunition Inventory | not yet mapped (withheld from public inspection) |

## Notes

- The PFA petition is large: plaintiff/defendant parties + a defendant-
  identifiers box, relationship, court/criminal history, children, the
  most-recent-incident statement, firearms (item 13), and an A-P list of relief
  requested. The Vault maps the petition; **Attachment A** (the firearms
  inventory) is a separate sheet, not yet mapped.
- The PA intake section fills the PA-specific items — PA's relief list (A-P) is
  its own, distinct from the other states'. See `coverage.md`.
- Drop the official blank fillable PDFs here for lea-be-core's renderer. Source:
  pacourts.us / PA AOPC forms.
