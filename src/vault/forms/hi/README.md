# HI order-for-protection forms

Blank, official Hawai'i Family Court forms for the HRS ch. 586 flow. Public
documents. **Blank templates only — never commit a form filled with a
survivor's information.**

| Form | Title | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1F-P-752A | Petition for an Order for Protection (SELF) | mapped (`form.py`) |
| 1F-P-752A (Legal Definitions) | reference definitions | static (no data) |

## Notes

- 1F-P-752A (HRS ch. 586) covers parties, the ch. 586 relationship basis, an
  acts-of-abuse checklist plus a harm-type classification, incident statements,
  firearms/electric guns, and a section II list of TRO + protective-order relief.
- The HI intake section fills the HI-specific items — HI's abuse-act, harm-type,
  and relief lists are its own. See `coverage.md`.
- The petition is the Ex Parte TRO petition; the form is for the **First
  Circuit (O'ahu)** — confirm the circuit for non-O'ahu filings.
- Drop the official blank fillable PDFs here for lea-be-core's renderer. Source:
  courts.state.hi.us forms.
